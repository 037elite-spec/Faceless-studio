Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Summary

Faceless Studio runs on the user's own computer. It does not have servers, does not collect data for the owner, and never sells or shares data with anyone.

2. Data the app accesses from TikTok

Only after you authorize it, and only for your own connected accounts:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): display name, avatar and account ID, to show which account is connected.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): followers, likes and number of videos, to show your progress.
  • Your videos (video.list): titles, views and likes of your recent videos, to show their performance.
  • Uploading (video.upload, video.publish): to send the videos you choose to your account or drafts.

3. Where data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only in a local folder on your computer. They are not sent to the owner or to any third party. The app communicates only with the official TikTok and YouTube APIs.

4. How long it is kept

Data stays on your computer until you disconnect the account or delete the app folder. Disconnecting an account deletes its tokens.

5. Your choices

You can revoke access at any time in the app or in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). You can delete all local data by deleting the app's data folder.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version. Contact: github.com/037elite-spec
