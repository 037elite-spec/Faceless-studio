Faceless Studio

A personal desktop tool to create short videos and publish them to my own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What it does

  • Helps me plan and produce original short-form videos (script, narration, images and captions) on my own computer.
  • Lets me connect my own TikTok accounts with TikTok Login Kit and upload my finished videos with the Content Posting API.
  • Shows basic statistics of my own accounts (followers, likes, recent videos) so I can track their progress.

Who it is for

Faceless Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sell anything.
